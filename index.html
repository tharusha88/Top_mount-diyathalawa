Leave the city below the mist.

Diyatalawa Sri Lanka

Top Mountain is a 3-acre estate in the cool, pine-covered hills of Diyatalawa: wooden cabanas, a clay-walled mud house, villas and a whole bungalow, spread down a green hillside. Tea on the balcony, mist lifting off the hills, and a whole estate to wander.
